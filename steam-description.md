[h1]Description of Mod[/h1]
[b]Outfit Stands Plus Forked[/b] makes outfit stands easier to use while keeping gear transfers safer around other RimWorld mods that also manage apparel or movement.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Custom Naming:[/b] Name stands automatically from the assigned pawn or manually as needed.
[*][b]Stand Variants:[/b] Supports adult, kid, mechanized, and mending stand variants.
[*][b]Shared Gizmos:[/b] Use a single multi-pawn command for selected eligible pawns.
[*][b]Live Apparel Sync:[/b] Keeps stand apparel policy behavior aligned with the selected policy set.
[*][b]Safer Transfers:[/b] Skips invalid or incompatible items instead of dropping or duplicating them.
[*][b]Optional Integrations:[/b] Works with Gear Up And Go and Defensive Positions Forked when those mods are loaded.
[/list]

[h1]How to Use[/h1]
Assign a pawn to a stand, then use the stand gizmos to rename it, swap apparel, or trigger the stand's gear flow.

[h1]Compatibilities[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Supported RimWorld version:[/b] 1.6.
[*][b]Required mod:[/b] Harmony.
[*][b]Additional required content:[/b] Odyssey.
[*][b]Optional integrations:[/b] Gear Up And Go and Defensive Positions Forked.
[*][b]Load order:[/b] Load after Odyssey and Harmony.
[/list]

[h1]Settings / Setup Instructions[/h1]
The mod is driven mostly by the stand gizmos and the assigned pawn settings. No separate setup tool is required for normal use.

[h1]Description[/h1]
[b]Outfit Stands Plus Forked[/b] improves outfit stand assignment and gear flow while protecting apparel and weapons from unsafe transfers around other mods.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Custom naming:[/b] Name stands automatically from the assigned pawn or rename them manually.
[*][b]Stand variants:[/b] Supports adult, child, mechanized, and mending stand variants.
[*][b]Shared group controls:[/b] Use one command for multiple selected eligible pawns while preserving each pawn's Swap or Return mode.
[*][b]Live apparel-policy sync:[/b] Refresh stand filters from selected apparel policies.
[*][b]Single-pawn assignment:[/b] Keep each stand assigned to one pawn and repair older multi-pawn assignments.
[*][b]Safer transfers:[/b] Skip missing, forbidden, locked, forced, incompatible, or otherwise invalid gear instead of dropping or duplicating it.
[*][b]Optional integrations:[/b] Coordinate with Gear Up And Go and Defensive Positions Forked when loaded.
[/list]

[h1]How to Use[/h1]
Assign a pawn to a stand, choose the stand's apparel policies, and use its gizmos to rename it or control gear flow. For several selected pawns, use the shared assigned-stands command and its right-click options to choose Swap or Return behavior.

[h1]Settings and Configuration[/h1]
The mod uses stand gizmos, assignments, and selected apparel policies. No separate configuration utility is required for normal use.

[h1]Compatibility, Load Order, Multiplayer, and Save Safety[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Load order:[/b] Load after Odyssey and Harmony.
[*][b]Optional integrations:[/b] Gear Up And Go and Defensive Positions Forked.
[*][b]Transfer safety:[/b] Stand plans are validated again at execution time so stale changes from other mods are skipped safely.
[*][b]Job safety:[/b] Automatic stand jobs do not start while another supported gear-flow or movement job owns the pawn.
[/list]

[h1]Fork History[/h1]
Outfit Stands Plus was created by khamenman. This fork keeps its stand workflow usable with RimWorld 1.6 and Odyssey while adding safer modern gear-flow behavior.

Compared with the upstream mod, this fork adds custom naming across stand variants, single-pawn assignment, shared multi-pawn controls, live apparel-policy syncing, transactional transfer validation, and integrations with Gear Up And Go and Defensive Positions Forked.
